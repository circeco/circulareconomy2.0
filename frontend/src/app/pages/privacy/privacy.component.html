Privacy

This section describes how Circeco handles information when you use the service.

Data controller

The data controller for personal information processed by Circeco is Piero Grilli, who can be contacted through the contact form on the Circeco home page.

What we collect

Account information

If you create a Circeco account, we process your email address and authentication information through Google Firebase Authentication.

Circeco does not store your password in plain text. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication.

An account allows you to sign in, save favourites, and access your account across supported devices.

Favourites

Places and events that you save as favourites are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and associated with your Circeco account.

This allows your favourites to remain available when you sign in on another supported device.

City and display preferences

Circeco stores the selected city and certain display preferences locally on your device using browser storage.

These preferences remain on your device unless you clear the relevant browser or application data.

Location

Circeco can use your device’s location to show your position on the map and provide location-dependent features such as nearby sorting.

Location access is optional. Circeco requests location permission only when a location-dependent feature is used and only after you allow it through your browser or device.

The current Circeco web application uses a one-time location request rather than continuous location tracking. Circeco does not use background location tracking.

If you do not allow location access, you can continue to use Circeco, although location-dependent features may not be available.

Location is used in connection with the city selected in Circeco. Circeco does not automatically replace the selected city catalogue with another city’s catalogue based solely on your location.

Maps

The Circeco atlas uses Mapbox to provide interactive maps.

When the atlas is loaded, your device may send requests to Mapbox to retrieve map resources such as map tiles, styles, fonts, or other required resources. These requests may include technical information such as your IP address and approximate geographic area.

Mapbox processes information according to its own privacy practices.

Place discovery and geographic data

Circeco uses OpenStreetMap and related services, including Overpass and Nominatim, for parts of its place-discovery and geographic-data workflow.

These services may process technical information associated with requests made to their infrastructure.

Information obtained from these services may be processed and incorporated into Circeco’s public catalogue.

Contact form

If you contact Circeco through the contact form on the home page, the information you submit, such as your name, email address, and message, is sent to Formspree so that the message can be received and answered.

The contact form is not used to create a Circeco account automatically.

Contact-form submissions are not stored in Circeco’s Firestore database.

Public catalogue

Places and events displayed in the Circeco catalogue are public information intended to help users discover circular activities.

Information about public places, organisations, events, opening hours, websites, and similar information is not treated as private account information merely because it appears in Circeco.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide and operate Circeco;
  • authenticate users and manage accounts;
  • store and synchronise favourites;
  • provide maps and location-dependent functionality;
  • remember selected cities and display preferences;
  • receive and respond to contact requests;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service;
  • prevent misuse of accounts and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use advertising trackers on Circeco.

Legal bases

Where EU/EEA or UK data-protection laws apply, Circeco relies on the following legal bases where appropriate:

  • Contract: Account information and favourites are processed because they are necessary to provide the account features you request.
  • Consent: Optional device location is processed when you grant location permission.
  • Legitimate interests: Certain technical, security, maintenance, and service-operation activities may be processed where necessary for the legitimate operation and protection of Circeco, while respecting applicable legal requirements.
  • Communication: Information submitted through the contact form is processed so that Circeco can receive and respond to your request.

Where consent is the legal basis, you can withdraw that consent at any time, for example by disabling location permission through your browser or device settings.

Third-party services

Circeco relies on third-party services and data sources to operate parts of the service:

  • Google Firebase / Firebase Authentication — user authentication.
  • Google Cloud Firestore — storage of account-related favourites and application data.
  • Mapbox — interactive maps and related map resources.
  • Formspree — delivery of contact-form messages.
  • OpenStreetMap — geographic and map-related open data.
  • Overpass — geographic/place-data queries used in the Circeco data-discovery workflow.
  • Nominatim — geographic search and geocoding used in the Circeco data workflow.

These third parties may process technical or personal information according to their own policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

Some third parties may act as independent controllers for certain processing activities. Their own privacy policies may therefore apply to information they process directly.

International data transfers

Some third-party providers used by Circeco may process or store information outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including in countries such as the United States.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, Circeco relies on appropriate safeguards where required by applicable data-protection law, such as recognised contractual or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

Third-party providers may change their infrastructure, processing locations, or service arrangements. Their current privacy documentation should therefore also be consulted for detailed information about their processing activities.

How long we keep data

Account information and favourites are generally retained while your account remains active.

If you delete your account, Circeco will delete the account and associated favourites that Circeco controls, subject to information that must be retained where required by law or information that may remain temporarily in secure backups before being overwritten.

Browser storage containing city and display preferences remains on your device until you clear the relevant data or it is otherwise removed.

Contact-form messages are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request and operate or document the Circeco project, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Third-party providers may retain technical logs or other information according to their own retention policies.

Security

Circeco uses reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information processed to help protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication, and access to account-related Firestore information is controlled through application and database security rules.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Account deletion

You can delete your Circeco account through the account functionality provided by Circeco.

Deleting your account removes the account and associated favourites controlled by Circeco, subject to the retention exceptions described above.

The current web application provides account deletion through the account functionality.

If native iPhone or Android applications are introduced in the future, they will provide an appropriate account-deletion mechanism where required by applicable law and platform rules.

Cookies and local storage

Circeco uses browser storage to remember information such as your selected city and display preferences.

These technologies are used to provide and remember functionality and are not used by Circeco for advertising or cross-site behavioural tracking.

Third-party services such as Mapbox, Firebase, Formspree, OpenStreetMap-related services, Overpass, or Nominatim may use their own technical mechanisms when providing their services. Their respective policies describe those mechanisms in more detail.

Children

Circeco is not directed at children under 16.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Circeco, please contact us through the home page contact form.
