Privacy policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

This policy describes how Circeco (circeco.org) handles information when you use the website, the installable web app, and any future Circeco apps for iPhone or Android. Circeco helps people find circular places and events in a selected city.

Who is responsible

Circeco.org operates this service. Questions about privacy can be sent with the contact form on the home page.

What we collect

  • Account. If you sign up, we store your email and a password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication). We use this so you can sign in, save favourites, and manage your account.
  • Favourites. Places and events you save are stored in our database (Google Cloud Firestore) under your account, so they appear when you sign in on another device.
  • City and display preferences. The selected city and some display choices are kept in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers them. These stay on your device unless you clear site data.
  • Location. The atlas can show your position and nearby places only if you turn on “Use my location” and allow it in the browser or OS. We do not track you in the background. Location is used on the map for the city you already selected; we do not mix catalogues from another city.
  • Map. The map is provided by Mapbox. Loading the atlas sends map-tile requests to Mapbox (which may include your IP address and approximate area).
  • Contact form. If you send a message from the home page, your name, email, and message go to Formspree so we can reply. We do not use that form to create an account.
  • Public catalogue. Approved places and events are public information on the site. They are not your personal account data.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising trackers on the site today.

Legal bases (EU/EEA/UK)

If those laws apply: we process account and favourites data to provide the service you ask for (contract). We process optional location with your consent. We process contact-form messages to respond to you. Map delivery is required to show the atlas.

How long we keep data

Account and favourites stay until you delete the account or we close the service. Browser storage lasts until you clear it. Contact-form messages are kept only as long as needed to reply and run the project. Mapbox and Firebase keep technical logs under their own policies.

Processors

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database
  • Mapbox — map tiles and map style
  • Formspree — contact form delivery

Each of those providers has its own privacy policy for data they process on our behalf or as a separate controller of technical logs.

Your choices

  • You can use the atlas and events without an account.
  • You can refuse location permission. Nearby sorting will not run.
  • Signed-in users can change email or password, and can delete the account in Account (this removes that account and its favourites).
  • You can ask us, via the home page contact form, for a copy of account data we hold or to correct it.

Children

Circeco is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Apps and updates

A future Circeco app in the App Store or Google Play uses this same service and this same policy. We will update this page if that changes. The date at the top will change when we do.
